01 — Foundation

The brand, modernized

v2 keeps every brand-book rule — the purple / orange / brown / cream palette and the AllRoundGothic + FilsonPro pairing — and pushes them forward with a bolder type scale, mono-style micro-labels, vivid full-bleed surfaces and conversion-first components inspired by the strongest CRO reference in the category.

Seductive

Engaging & provocative

Copy sparks curiosity and desire. Warm, rounded letterforms and an italic accent voice carry the flirtation — never explicit.

Sophisticated

Elegant & suggestive

Bold without vulgarity. Generous whitespace, confident scale and a disciplined token set keep every screen premium.

Healthy

Honest & natural

Clear about natural ingredients and trust. FDA-compliant, made in the USA, satisfaction guaranteed — surfaced throughout.

Tokens below are written as CSS custom properties so the dev team can map them 1:1 onto the Tailwind @theme already present in src/app/globals.css.

Our proprietary blend of natural aphrodisiac botanicals — maca, ginger, L-theanine and fenugreek — is folded into premium Arriba cocoa for a gradual, sensory effect designed to be shared.

Is it safe and natural?

Yes — Dips is made in the USA from natural ingredients and is FDA-compliant. Every box ships with a satisfaction guarantee.

How is it shipped?

Discreetly and securely. The 3-box bundle ships free; smaller orders use flat-rate shipping shown at checkout.
